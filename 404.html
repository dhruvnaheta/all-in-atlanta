Our Mission

Atlanta Poker for Everyone

Our mission is to build an inclusive poker community where everyone feels welcome at the table, with a special focus on teaching more women the game, creating opportunities to learn, and making poker more approachable for new players.

All In Atlanta is a free-to-play poker league bringing players together every Monday night at Wicked Wolf, every Wednesday night at 5 Paces, and every Thursday night at Wicked Wolf.

No buy-ins, no entry fees. Just register on the Active Games page when check-in opens, show up, and play. Earn points for every finish, climb the monthly leaderboard, and compete for prizes in separate quarterly championships. Qualification details are announced before each championship.

We're just getting started — more venues and nights coming as the league grows.

League Rules

View Eligibility & Promotions →
Entry & Registration
  • All In Atlanta games are 100% free. No buy-in or entry fee.
  • Players register via the Active Games page when check-in is open.
  • All players must be 21+ and present at the venue for the duration of play.
  • No purchase is required to play. If you choose to buy something, it is a courtesy to our hosting partners.
Format
  • All games are Texas Hold'em No-Limit tournaments.
  • Game cutoff is 1 hour 40 minutes after the posted start time.
  • Starting chip stacks are equal for all players, announced before play begins.
  • Blind levels escalate every 15 minutes as shown on the timer.
Table Rules
  • Standard Texas Hold'em hand rankings apply.
  • Players must act in turn. Acting out of turn may result in a penalty.
  • Verbal declarations are binding when made in turn.
  • All bets and raises must be at least the size of the big blind.
  • String bets are not allowed.
All-In & Side Pots
  • A player may go all-in for their total remaining chips at any time.
  • Side pots are created when an all-in player cannot match a full bet.
  • All called players must show cards at showdown.
Points Awarded
  • 1st: 10 pts
  • 2nd: 7 pts
  • 3rd: 5 pts
  • 4th: 12 pts
  • 5th: 10 pts
  • 6th: 8 pts
  • 7th: 6 pts
  • 8th: 4 pts
  • All other registered players receive 1 participation point.
  • Every player who reaches the final table earns more points than any player who did not.
  • Monthly standings start fresh on the first of each month in Atlanta. All-time standings preserve points across every month. Quarterly championships are separate events; qualification details will be announced before each championship.
Tiebreakers
  • Ranking ties broken by 1st place finishes, then 2nd, and so on, within the selected period and series. Exact ties share a rank (1, 1, 3).
Expected Behavior
  • Treat all players, hosts, and venue staff with respect at all times.
  • All In Atlanta has zero tolerance for discrimination. We are committed to creating an inclusive environment at every table; anyone we determine is not supporting that environment may be asked to leave.
  • Excessive taunting or poor sportsmanship may result in removal.
  • Discussing your hand during a live round is prohibited except in all-in situations.
  • Collusion of any kind is grounds for immediate disqualification and ban.

Eligibility & Promotions

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN

All In Atlanta tournaments are 100% free to enter and play. No purchase is necessary to participate or win a prize.

There are no buy-ins, entry fees, or re-buys permitted at any All In Atlanta tournament. Every participant receives a baseline starting chip stack completely free of charge upon registration.

All In Atlanta events are structured as free-to-play amusement promotions and are intended to operate in compliance with applicable Georgia law, including O.C.G.A. § 16-12-21.

Players must be 21 years of age or older to participate.

How It Works

FREE ENTRY → FREE STARTING STACK → PLAY TEXAS HOLD'EM → WIN VENUE-PROVIDED PRIZES
$0 BUY-IN • $0 ENTRY FEE • NO RE-BUYS • NO PLAYER-FUNDED PRIZE POOL

No player is required to spend money to participate in an All In Atlanta tournament. Players do not contribute money toward a prize pool, and losing a tournament does not result in the loss of money or anything else of value wagered on the game.

Bonus Chip Rules

All players receive a baseline starting stack completely free of charge. Players may also have opportunities to receive promotional bonus chips through both purchase and non-purchase promotional activities.

Any eligible venue purchases that qualify for promotional bonus chips may only be redeemed for bonus chips during the first five rounds of tournament play.

Purchase-based promotional bonus chips are also subject to a maximum cap per player, per tournament. Once a player reaches the applicable cap, additional purchases will not result in additional tournament chips.

Purchases are always optional. No purchase is required to enter, play, remain in the tournament, or be eligible to win a prize.

Free methods of obtaining bonus chips are available to all players.

Free Ways to Earn Bonus Chips

Players have multiple opportunities to earn additional tournament chips without making a purchase.

Arrive Early

Arrive and check in before 8:00 PM to receive bonus chips.

Bring New Players

Bring a new player to an All In Atlanta event and receive bonus chips.

There is no limit to the number of new players you may bring. Bonus chips may be earned for each eligible new player you introduce to the league.

Deal for Your Table

Volunteer to deal for your table and receive bonus chips for helping run the tournament.

Wear All In Atlanta Merch

Wear eligible All In Atlanta merchandise you already own to the event and receive bonus chips.

Purchasing merchandise is never required to participate in an All In Atlanta tournament.

Post on Social Media

Post about All In Atlanta on social media and tag All In Atlanta to receive bonus chips in accordance with the applicable tournament rules.

Alternative Free Method

Additional non-purchase methods of obtaining bonus chips are available upon request from the Tournament Director.

No Purchase Required

Purchasing food, beverages, merchandise, or other products from All In Atlanta or a host venue is completely optional and is not a condition of tournament participation.

Certain eligible purchases may qualify for promotional bonus chips; however, purchase-based bonus chips are limited to the first five rounds of play and are subject to a maximum per-player cap.

No purchase is required to:

  • Enter an All In Atlanta tournament
  • Receive your baseline starting chip stack
  • Remain in the tournament
  • Be eligible to win a prize
  • Access opportunities to earn bonus chips

Alternative non-purchase methods of obtaining bonus chips are available upon request from the Tournament Director.

Tournament Chips Have No Cash Value

All In Atlanta tournament chips are provided solely for use during tournament play and have no cash value.

Tournament chips may not be purchased directly, sold, redeemed for cash, exchanged for money or other things of value, or transferred between players except where expressly permitted by official tournament rules.

All forms of player-to-player wagering and side betting are prohibited at All In Atlanta events.

Prizes

All prizes offered at All In Atlanta tournaments are posted and provided by the participating host venues. Prizes are not funded through player buy-ins, entry fees, re-buys, or player contributions.

Players do not contribute money to a poker pot or player-funded prize pool. No portion of a player's tournament participation constitutes a wager, and no purchase is necessary to be eligible to win a prize.

Prize amounts and/or types may vary by event and venue. The applicable prizes for each tournament are determined, posted, and provided by the participating host venue.

Player Eligibility & Conduct

All participants must be 21 years of age or older.

By participating in an All In Atlanta event, players agree to follow the official tournament rules and applicable rules of the host venue.

The following are prohibited:

  • Cash wagering or side betting
  • Paying an entry fee or buy-in
  • Purchasing re-buys
  • Buying or selling tournament chips between players
  • Redeeming tournament chips for cash
  • Collusion or cheating
  • Attempting to convert an All In Atlanta tournament into a cash game

The Tournament Director reserves the right to disqualify any participant who violates tournament or venue rules.

Georgia Law & Compliance

All In Atlanta structures its events as free-to-play promotional/amusement poker tournaments. No buy-in, entry fee, or re-buy is required or permitted, and every participant receives a free baseline starting stack.

Players may receive promotional bonus chips through eligible activities. Free, non-purchase methods of obtaining bonus chips are available, including alternative methods available upon request from the Tournament Director. Purchase-based promotional bonus chips are limited to the first five rounds of play and are subject to a maximum per-player cap.

Prizes are posted and provided by participating host venues and are not funded by player entry fees, buy-ins, re-buys, or contributions to a prize pool.

All In Atlanta intends to conduct its events in compliance with applicable Georgia law, including O.C.G.A. § 16-12-21.

Nothing on this page should be interpreted as permitting wagering, cash games, side betting, or any other activity prohibited by applicable law.

Questions?

Questions regarding tournament rules, promotional bonus chips, free methods of obtaining bonus chips, or the All In Atlanta tournament format may be directed to the Tournament Director at any All In Atlanta event.
